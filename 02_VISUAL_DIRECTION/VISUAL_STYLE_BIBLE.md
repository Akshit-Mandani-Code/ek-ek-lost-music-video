# Visual Style Bible

## Core visual identity

Photorealistic, grounded, emotionally intimate cinematic drama with restrained surrealism. The film should feel like a carefully directed live-action short, not a montage of unrelated AI clips. Prioritize human emotion, motivated composition and continuity over spectacle.

## Cinematography

- Use motivated compositions and clear visual storytelling; every frame should communicate a story beat.
- Favor close-ups for emotional details and medium/wide frames for isolation and spatial relationships.
- Keep camera movement controlled and physically plausible. Locked-off frames are valid when stillness is meaningful.
- Use naturalistic perspective and believable depth of field; avoid extreme wide-angle facial distortion.
- Preserve screen direction and camera geography within each location.
- Keep actions simple enough for short image-to-video clips.
- Avoid excessive slow motion, artificial lens flares, glowing particles, fantasy effects and gratuitous drone shots.
- Use visual inserts sparingly; do not turn the film into a slideshow of props.

## Lighting and color

**Present-day struggle — approved direction:** cool, muted, low-saturation neutral tones. Maintain skin tone; do not make the image cyan or lifeless.

**Memory fragments — approved direction:** subtly warmer and more natural, but not every memory should be golden or nostalgic.

**Newborn memory — approved direction:** intimate, soft and gently warm practical/daylight. It is an actual remembered past, not a supernatural vision.

**Final present — approved direction:** gradually soften and warm slightly without a visible magical color transformation.

Exact LUT, contrast curve and color values are not decided. Choose a consistent grade during editing rather than baking extreme looks into generated images.

## Composition and aspect

Compose for a cinematic 16:9 master. Keep the main subject and essential story information safely inside the frame for a 1080p edit. Generate at the highest genuinely free usable resolution; do not upscale just to claim a larger number.

## Texture and realism

Natural skin texture, believable fabric, real water behavior, coherent reflections, realistic hands and domestic materials. Avoid plastic skin, over-sharpening, excessive film grain, fake bokeh, malformed architecture and artificial beauty retouching.

## Continuity rules

1. Written character bibles are the canonical identity source. Generate character sheets from text; no existing portrait is required.
2. Keep the same charcoal/washed-black pullover hoodie: no zipper, logo, text or graphic.
3. Hood is up in bathroom, faceless and present-day station scenes; down naturally behind neck/shoulders in the mother-lap ending.
4. Keep the same Indian middle-class home design and consistent bathroom/bedroom geography.
5. Props must remain recognizable across inserts: diary, phone, laptop, offer/university papers, medical reports and hackathon award.
6. Facelessness is a controlled visual metaphor. Do not mutate the skull, hood, body or clothing. Achieve the effect through framing, reflection, shadow, editing or compositing—not an uncontrolled AI face morph.
7. The newborn memory is a real past event, not a dream realm, hallucination or supernatural vision.

## AI video movement language

Prefer one primary movement per clip: a subtle push-in, a small head/hand movement, falling water, a breath, a tear, or moving background extras. Avoid asking a short clip to perform multiple complex actions. Preserve identity, clothing, architecture, lighting and composition throughout the clip. Use a cut or an edit for major story-state changes instead of asking a video model to morph faces or locations.

## Reconstruction rule

Every shot prompt must contain enough relevant character, wardrobe, environment and cinematography detail to work without an attached reference image. Internally generated character/location sheets may be reused within a future session, but they are optional working aids rather than external inputs.
