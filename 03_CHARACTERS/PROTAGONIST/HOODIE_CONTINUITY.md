# Hoodie Continuity — Approved

## Garment lock
One plain charcoal / washed-black cotton pullover hoodie. No zipper, branding, lettering, graphic or decorative details. Relaxed but not oversized fit, matte fabric, consistent hood shape and drawstrings (if present, keep their design consistent). This written description is canonical; no wardrobe reference image is required.

## Three story states
1. **Bathroom water-splash:** hood up, hair concealed. The face remains visible initially. Water and mirror reflection carry the emotional action.
2. **Faceless protagonist:** same hoodie, hood still up. Facial erasure is a separate visual metaphor; do not turn the hood into a black void or alter head anatomy.
3. **Mother-lap ending:** same hoodie, hood down and naturally gathered behind neck/shoulders. Hairline and scalp are exposed so the mother's tear lands on skin/hair, not fabric.

## Symbolic progression
Hiding → anonymity → safety / being seen.

## Generation continuity checklist
- Same garment color, material, fit and hood construction in every scene.
- No accidental zipper, logos, text, extra layers or color changes.
- Hood position must match the scene state.
- Recreate the protagonist's identity from the written character bible; do not require CH3 or another external portrait.
- In the final scene, do not generate a different shirt/hoodie; only the hood position changes.
- If an AI platform supports internal image references, it may reuse a character sheet generated during that same recreation session.
