# Mother Character Bible

## Identity
Indian mother, approximately 53–58 years old. Natural, recognizable Indian features with subtle signs of age. Her appearance should feel like a believable woman from an urban Indian middle-class family, not a fashion model or an exaggerated screen stereotype.

## Appearance and presence
- Age range: approximately 53–58.
- Natural facial proportions and visible, subtle signs of age.
- Warm, observant eyes; expressive through small changes rather than exaggerated facial acting.
- Hair kept neatly tied back in the present-day sequence.
- Understated, practical presentation; avoid heavy glamour makeup or ornate styling.
- Keep her recognizable facial identity consistent between present-day and remembered past. Any age difference should be plausible and subtle.

## Character-sheet generation instructions
A future AI must generate its own mother character sheet from this written description before producing story shots. Create a neutral front-facing portrait, three-quarter view and side profile, plus restrained emotional expressions (quiet concern, tenderness, remembered joy). Use a coherent, natural Indian appearance and preserve the same facial identity across views. If the generation platform supports internal image references, reuse this newly generated sheet during the same recreation session. If not, repeat the written identity and styling in each relevant prompt. No external mother photograph is required.

## Wardrobe
**Present day:** simple elegant cotton saree, understated jewellery, tied-back hair. Muted, natural colors; avoid ornate bridal styling.

**Newborn memory:** simple traditional clothing and a subtly different, era-appropriate hairstyle. Maintain facial continuity. Do not make the memory look like a fantasy or a heavily stylized period film.

## Personality and performance
Strong but silently suffering. She hides her own pain to remain a source of strength for her son. Emotion is communicated through eyes, pauses, small gestures and restrained touch. Avoid exaggerated crying or melodrama.

## Final sequence performance
- Adult son rests with his head in her lap.
- She closes her eyes and remembers holding him as a newborn in the same lap.
- In the newborn memory, her lips move silently as if giving a blessing; no audible dialogue.
- On return to present, her eyes remain closed.
- One tear falls naturally onto the adult son's exposed hairline/scalp.
- Do not add glowing effects or supernatural visual treatment.
- Her movement should remain minimal and physically believable; the emotion comes from the memory and the tear, not theatrical gestures.
