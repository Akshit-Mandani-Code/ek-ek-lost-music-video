# Mother Character Bible

## Canonical identity

An Indian mother from an urban middle-class family, approximately 53–58 years old. She is emotionally strong and quietly suffering; she hides her own pain to remain a source of strength for her son. She must feel like a believable person, never a melodramatic screen stereotype or fashion model.

## Appearance

- Age: approximately 53–58 in the present-day story.
- Natural Indian facial features and realistic skin texture, with subtle, age-appropriate lines.
- Warm, observant eyes; emotion is carried primarily through the eyes and small changes in expression.
- Hair neatly tied back in the present-day sequence.
- Understated, practical grooming; no heavy glamour makeup, ornate styling or bridal look.
- Natural, average build and believable posture.
- Do not invent prominent scars, tattoos, spectacles or distinctive jewellery unless later approved.

Preserve a recognizable facial identity between present-day and newborn-memory scenes. The memory is earlier in her life, so allow only plausible age reduction and a modestly different hairstyle. Do not turn her into a different person.

## Wardrobe

**Present day — approved:** simple elegant cotton saree, muted natural color, understated jewellery, hair tied back. Avoid ornate bridal styling, heavy embroidery or conspicuous luxury.

**Newborn memory — approved direction:** simple traditional clothing suitable to the earlier period, with a subtly different, era-appropriate hairstyle. Exact saree color and jewellery are not yet locked; keep them simple and visually related to the present-day character.

## Personality and performance

Strong but silently suffering. She has noticed her son's struggle but does not force a conversation or perform exaggerated consolation. Use small gestures, attentive eyes, pauses and gentle touch. Avoid melodramatic crying, dramatic hand gestures or a big staged smile.

## Final sequence — locked performance

1. Adult son rests with his head in her lap.
2. Mother looks at him with quiet tenderness and closes her eyes.
3. Cut to an actual memory of her holding him as a newborn in the same lap.
4. In the memory, her lips move silently as if giving a blessing; there is no audible dialogue.
5. Return to the present. Her eyes remain closed.
6. One natural tear falls onto the adult son's exposed hairline/scalp, not onto the hoodie.
7. Her movement remains minimal and physically believable.

The tear is not magical: no glow, sparkle, supernatural trail or visual effect. The emotion comes from the match between the remembered newborn and the adult son resting in the same lap.

## Continuity checklist

- Same recognizable facial identity in present and memory.
- Present-day age remains approximately 53–58.
- Present hair tied back; memory hairstyle subtly earlier.
- Present saree understated cotton; memory clothing simple and traditional.
- Restrained performance and natural skin texture.
