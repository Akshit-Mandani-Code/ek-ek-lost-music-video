## Master image prompt

Create a clean, photorealistic live-action character reference sheet for one believable Indian mother, approximately 55 years old, from an urban middle-class family. She is emotionally strong and quietly suffering, with warmth and concern expressed through her eyes rather than exaggerated acting.

**Identity:** natural Indian facial features, realistic medium-brown skin with age-appropriate texture and subtle lines, warm observant eyes, natural facial proportions, average build. She should look like a real person, not a fashion model, celebrity or screen stereotype. Do not invent prominent scars, tattoos, spectacles or distinctive jewellery.

**Present-day styling:** simple elegant muted-color cotton saree, understated jewellery, hair neatly tied back. Avoid bridal styling, heavy embroidery, heavy makeup or luxury fashion. Keep the saree practical and believable.

**Layout:** one single wide 16:9 reference sheet on a simple neutral warm-grey studio background. Show the same woman in clearly separated, evenly sized views: front-facing head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter portrait, side profile and one natural waist-up standing view. Include three restrained expression references: quiet concern, tender affection and remembered joy. Preserve the same face and age across all panels.

**Lighting and camera:** soft neutral studio light, natural skin texture, eye-level camera, normal perspective, no wide-angle distortion. This is a character reference sheet, not a dramatic poster.

**Strict constraints:** no text labels, watermark, logos, extra people, duplicate faces, different identities, glamour retouching, exaggerated crying, ornate jewellery, bridal clothing, fantasy styling, illustration, anime or 3D render.

## Memory-age continuity note

This sheet establishes the present-day identity. For the newborn-memory sequence, create a younger version of this same woman by reducing age subtly and plausibly, preserving recognizable facial structure and eyes. Use simple traditional clothing and a modestly different earlier hairstyle. Do not generate a new unrelated woman.

- [ ] Simple muted cotton saree and understated jewellery.

- [ ] Restrained expressions; no melodrama.
- [ ] Photorealistic and suitable as an internal reference.
