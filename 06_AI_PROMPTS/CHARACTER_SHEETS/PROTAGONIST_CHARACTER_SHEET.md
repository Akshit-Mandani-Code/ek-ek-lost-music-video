## Master image prompt

Create a clean, photorealistic live-action character reference sheet for one believable 27-year-old Indian man from an urban middle-class background. He is a skilled computer engineer, with an understated everyday appearance rather than a fashion-model look.

**Identity must remain consistent in every view:** medium Indian brown skin tone with natural texture; oval face with naturally proportioned features; dark brown eyes; short, slightly messy black hair; subtle light stubble; average build. His face should feel ordinary and authentic, not beautified, not overly symmetrical and not given an exaggerated jawline. Do not invent scars, tattoos, spectacles, piercings or distinctive accessories.

**Wardrobe:** one plain charcoal / washed-black cotton pullover hoodie, relaxed but not oversized, matte fabric, no zipper, logo, lettering, print or graphics. For this character sheet show the hood DOWN so the face, hairline and haircut are clearly established. The hoodie must be the same garment later used with the hood up. Keep its neckline, seams, cuffs and material consistent.

**Layout:** one single wide 16:9 reference sheet on a simple neutral grey studio background, containing the same man in clearly separated, evenly sized views: front-facing head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter head-and-shoulders portrait, clean side-profile head-and-shoulders portrait, and one natural waist-up standing view. Add three small restrained expression references: neutral/tired, confused/overwhelmed, and quietly relieved. Every panel must depict the same person with matching facial proportions, skin tone, eye color, hairline, stubble and hoodie.

**Lighting and camera:** soft neutral studio light, natural skin texture, realistic photographic detail, eye-level camera, normal perspective, no wide-angle distortion, no dramatic colored lighting. The purpose is identity documentation, not a dramatic movie poster.

**Strict constraints:** no text labels, no watermark, no logos, no extra people, no duplicate heads within a panel, no different hairstyles, no facial hair changes, no wardrobe changes, no beauty retouching, no exaggerated expressions, no stylized illustration, no anime, no 3D character render. Maintain realistic Indian facial features and human anatomy.

## Selection checklist

- [ ] One consistent identity across all views.
- [ ] Medium Indian brown skin tone, oval face, dark brown eyes.
- [ ] Short slightly messy black hair and light stubble.
- [ ] Average build; believable everyday appearance.
- [ ] Plain charcoal pullover hoodie, hood down for this sheet.
- [ ] No invented distinctive features or accessories.
- [ ] Photorealistic, neutral and useful as an internal reference.
