## Model-agnostic master prompt

Create a photorealistic cinematic film still featuring a young Indian man, approximately 27 years old, from an urban middle-class family. He has a medium Indian skin tone, an oval face, naturally proportioned features, dark brown eyes, short slightly messy black hair (mostly concealed by the hood), subtle light stubble, and an average build. His appearance should feel like a believable young computer engineer, not a fashion model. Keep facial proportions natural and consistent with this written identity in all shots generated for this film.

**WARDROBE:**
He wears one plain charcoal-black / washed-black cotton pullover hoodie. It has no zipper, logo, lettering, print or graphics. Relaxed but not oversized fit, matte cotton texture, natural folds. The hood is UP and covers his hair naturally. This is the same hoodie used throughout the present-day story. Do not change its color, cut or material.

**SCENE:**
A realistic, comfortable urban Indian middle-class apartment bathroom. The man stands directly in front of a practical washbasin and mirror. He has just brought water to his face and is gently splashing it with both hands. Realistic water droplets are visible around his face and hands. The bathroom is clean and lived-in, modestly spacious, with neutral-toned surfaces and ordinary domestic fixtures; avoid luxury-hotel styling.

**EXPRESSION AND EMOTION:**
Emotionally exhausted, mentally overwhelmed and quietly distressed. His expression is natural and restrained, as if he is trying to regain control. No theatrical crying, exaggerated anguish or melodramatic expression.

**CINEMATOGRAPHY:**
Medium close-up. Camera positioned slightly behind and to one side of his shoulder, allowing both his physical face and its reflection to be visible in the bathroom mirror. Natural perspective, believable mirror geometry, cinematic composition and shallow depth of field. Keep the face and water action readable.

**LIGHTING AND COLOR:**
Soft, cool-neutral bathroom lighting with subtle shadows. Realistic skin texture and controlled highlights on water droplets. Muted, restrained color grading; no stylized neon or extreme teal-orange treatment.

**VISUAL STYLE:**
High-end photorealistic Hollywood psychological drama, restrained cinematic realism, natural skin and fabric detail, subtle film grain, believable Indian domestic environment.

**FORMAT:**
16:9 widescreen cinematic composition.

**STRICT CONSTRAINTS:**
- Keep the protagonist's written identity consistent; do not beautify or turn him into a different archetype.
- Keep the same plain charcoal pullover hoodie; hood must remain UP and naturally conceal the hair.
- Do not introduce other people.
