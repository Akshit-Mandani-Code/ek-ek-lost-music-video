## ENV-001 — Apartment bathroom

**Prompt:** Create a photorealistic wide 16:9 cinematic location reference of a believable, comfortable urban Indian middle-class apartment bathroom. Show a practical washbasin with counter, a single clear mirror, ordinary tap and domestic fixtures, neutral wall finish and soft practical lighting. Clean and maintained, modestly spacious, not luxurious. The space should feel like a real home bathroom, not a hotel or spa. Establish a readable layout with the mirror, basin and tap clearly related spatially. Keep counter clutter minimal. Natural materials, believable reflections, realistic proportions, restrained cool-neutral palette. No people, no text, no logos, no luxury fittings, no dramatic colored lighting, no fantasy effects.

**Lock after selection:** mirror shape and dimensions, basin/counter shape, tap design, wall finish, fixture positions, light-source direction and camera geography.

## ENV-002 — Bedroom / study

**Prompt:** Create a photorealistic wide 16:9 cinematic location reference of a young Indian computer engineer's bedroom/study in a comfortable middle-class apartment. The room is organized but slightly neglected, personal and lived-in. Include a practical desk and chair, one contemporary understated laptop, one phone, a few technical books/notes, a desk lamp and space for a diary and a small achievement memento. Use neutral walls, modest wooden furniture and believable domestic light. Avoid gaming-room RGB lighting, luxury furniture, excessive clutter or poverty stereotypes. No person, no readable text, no logos, no watermark.

**Lock after selection:** room layout, desk orientation, chair position, window/door positions, wall and floor finish, desk lamp, laptop/phone appearance and practical light sources.

## ENV-003 — Transit station (location choice still required)

**Prompt:** Create a photorealistic wide 16:9 cinematic location reference of a busy Indian railway station concourse or foot overbridge, grounded in contemporary everyday Indian urban life. Show convincing structural geometry, practical signage kept small and non-focal, natural crowd pathways and believable station lighting. Composition should offer a clear area where one stationary person could stand while commuters pass around him. Avoid malformed prominent text, cinematic disaster, panic, luxury airport styling or a Western station design. No featured protagonist, no readable brand names, no watermark.

**Important:** The exact station type is not yet approved. Choose either a railway station concourse/platform environment or a foot overbridge before generation, then lock it. Do not mix both layouts across shots.

**Lock after selection:** station type, architecture, floor/platform geometry, railings, signage style, light sources, crowd direction and protagonist standing position.

## ENV-004 — Mother-lap present-day room

**Prompt:** Create a photorealistic wide 16:9 cinematic location reference of a quiet, modestly comfortable living-room corner in the same urban Indian middle-class apartment. Include one believable sofa or cushioned seat with enough room for an adult son to rest his head naturally in his mother's lap. Use neutral walls, modest wooden furniture, restrained decor and soft practical/daylight. The room should feel intimate, safe and lived-in, not luxurious. Keep the composition uncluttered and leave clear space for two people. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark.

**Lock after selection:** exact seat/sofa, cushion color and shape, room angle, wall/furniture arrangement and light direction.

## ENV-005 — Recurring story props

Generate a separate clean product-style reference for each prop, on a neutral background:
- A contemporary A5 hardcover diary, matte charcoal/black/navy cover, cream/off-white pages, plain cover with no title or logo. This design remains provisional until selected.
- One understated contemporary laptop, no visible brand or readable screen text.
- One understated contemporary smartphone, no visible brand.
- One simple solo hackathon award/certificate/medal arrangement; exact design remains undecided.
- A small set of generic job/university/medical papers with no legible AI-generated body text. Any exact wording will be added in editing.

Keep each prop's scale, color, materials and visible wear consistent after selection. Do not generate a single collage if separate clean object references are possible.
