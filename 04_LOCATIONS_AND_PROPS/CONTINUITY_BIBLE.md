# Locations & Props Continuity Bible

This is the canonical written source for sets and recurring objects. All visual references must be generated from these descriptions during the recreation session. No location photograph, prop image or external reference is required.

## Overall home

A comfortable, well-maintained, slightly spacious urban Indian middle-class apartment. It is personal and lived-in, neither luxurious nor impoverished. Use neutral walls, practical domestic lighting, modest wooden furniture and a restrained palette. Keep architecture, wall finishes, flooring, doors and furniture coherent across all home scenes. Avoid hotel-like bathrooms, palatial rooms or generic Western suburban interiors.

## Bathroom — intro location

A believable apartment bathroom with a practical washbasin, mirror, tap and ordinary domestic fixtures. Clean and maintained but not luxurious. Neutral wall finish and soft practical lighting. The mirror, basin, tap, counter, wall finish and camera geography must remain identical across the intro shots.

The protagonist stands at the basin facing the mirror. The camera may see his physical face and reflection together. Water splash must obey gravity and remain physically plausible. Keep counter clutter minimal; no extra people or distracting toiletries. Exact mirror shape and fixture design may be chosen by the first generated location reference, then locked for all shots.

## Bedroom / study

An organized but slightly neglected personal room. It belongs to a young computer engineer and contains a desk, chair, laptop, phone, technical notes/books, desk lamp and a small number of story objects. The diary is a central storytelling device. Avoid a gaming-room stereotype, excessive RGB lighting, messy trash or an implausibly luxurious office.

Once the first reference is selected, lock desk orientation, chair position, window/door placement, wall finish, practical light sources and the position of recurring objects. Shoot inserts with consistent object scale and orientation.

## Transit station

A crowded Indian railway station or comparable Indian transit environment. The exact station type (railway station, foot overbridge or other transit space) remains **not decided** and must be selected before generating the station sequence. Once selected, preserve the same architecture, platform/overbridge geometry, signage treatment, lighting, crowd direction and camera axis.

The protagonist remains still while the crowd moves around him in a time-lapse / motion-blur contrast. Keep signage generic or accurately rendered; do not make malformed AI text a focal point. Do not add a train collision, danger, panic or unrelated incident.

## Mother-lap location

A quiet, modestly comfortable home interior that belongs to the same apartment. The mother and adult son sit/rest in a believable domestic setting. The newborn memory uses the same lap composition and a visually related environment, with a plausible earlier time period. The exact seat/sofa and room angle can be selected in the first reference and then locked.

## Recurring props

### Diary
Contemporary A5 hardcover, matte charcoal/black/navy cover and cream/off-white pages. This design is **provisional**, not yet approved. Not antique, ornate or distressed. It is a personal record of ambitions and thoughts; keep it consistent in size, cover and page color.

### Laptop and phone
Contemporary, understated devices. Choose one consistent laptop form/color and one phone form/color during reference generation. Avoid visible brands and legible screen text unless added later in editing.

### Hackathon achievement
Communicate a solo win through one coherent award/certificate/medal design. Exact design remains undecided. Do not create a team trophy or show a group win.

### Job offer and medical reports
Use a restrained paper insert: offer letter adjacent to medical reports/bills, communicating why the protagonist declined joining. Do not rely on generated small text; use designed post-production inserts if readable wording is needed.

### University admission and loan rejection
Use separate, controlled inserts to communicate admission followed by education-loan rejection. Keep any text generic/unreadable at generation stage and add accurate text in editing if required.

### Breakup and job departure
Possible evidence includes two coffee cups, a turned-down photograph, an office access card or a packed desk box. These are candidate visual devices, not all approved props. Select only what is necessary when individual shots are designed.

## Set and prop lock procedure

For each recurring set or prop:
1. Generate a reference from this written description.
2. Select one candidate that fits the story and realism.
3. Record its chosen visual traits in the relevant shot prompt or this bible.
4. Reuse the same reference internally when supported; otherwise repeat the written description.
5. Never let an optional generated image become the only record of a canonical design.
