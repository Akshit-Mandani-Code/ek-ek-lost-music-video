# Locations & Props Continuity Bible

This is the canonical written source for recreating sets and props. No location photographs, prop images or external references are required. Expand descriptions as creative choices are approved; do not let one generated image become an undocumented dependency.

## Home
A comfortable, well-maintained, slightly spacious urban Indian middle-class apartment. Neutral walls, wooden furniture, practical furnishings and lived-in details. The space should feel real and personal, not luxurious or impoverished. Maintain consistent architecture and material palette across all home scenes.

## Bedroom / study
Organized but slightly neglected. The diary is a central storytelling device. Desk elements may include a laptop, phone, technical notes/books, desk lamp, hackathon award and selected personal papers. Keep the set uncluttered enough for readable compositions. Lock desk orientation, furniture placement and practical light sources when detailed shot design begins.

## Bathroom
A believable home bathroom with washbasin, mirror, tap and practical lighting. Maintain consistent mirror shape, basin, tap, wall finish and camera geography across all intro shots. Water behavior must be physically plausible. It should belong to the same apartment, not look like a luxury hotel.

## Transit station
A crowded Indian railway station or comparable transit environment. The protagonist remains still while crowd movement creates a time-lapse contrast. Keep signage generic or accurately rendered; avoid malformed prominent text. The exact station type and layout remain to be locked during detailed visual development. Once chosen, reuse the same architecture, platform/overbridge geometry, lighting and crowd direction.

## Story props
- **Diary:** contemporary A5 hardcover; matte charcoal/black/navy cover and cream/off-white pages are provisional until the design is approved. Not antique or ornate.
- **Laptop and phone:** contemporary, understated, consistent across desk shots.
- **Hackathon award:** communicates a solo achievement; exact trophy/medal/certificate design remains undecided.
- **Job offer and medical reports:** use controlled inserts or post-production text for legibility; do not rely on AI-generated small text as a focal point.
- **University admission and loan rejection:** use designed inserts or carefully controlled close-ups rather than generated gibberish.
- **Breakup/job departure:** possible visual evidence includes two coffee cups, a turned-down photograph, access card or packed desk box. Select and lock exact props during shot design.

## Reconstruction rule
For every recurring set or prop, write down shape, scale, materials, colors, condition, placement and narrative function before generating dependent shots. Generate any reference sheets from these text descriptions within the current recreation session; never require an existing image.
