# Locations & Props Continuity Bible

This document will grow as reference stills and locations are approved. Do not invent a new set or prop design between shots without logging the change.

## Home
A comfortable, well-maintained, slightly spacious urban Indian middle-class apartment. Neutral walls, wooden furniture, practical furnishings and lived-in details. The space should feel real and personal, not luxurious or impoverished.

## Bedroom / study
Organized but slightly neglected. The diary is a central storytelling device. Desk elements may include a laptop, phone, technical notes/books, desk lamp, hackathon award and selected personal papers. Keep the set uncluttered enough for readable compositions.

## Bathroom
A believable home bathroom with washbasin, mirror, tap and practical lighting. Maintain consistent mirror shape, basin, tap, wall finish and camera geography across all intro shots. Water behavior must be physically plausible.

## Transit station
Crowded Indian railway station or comparable transit environment. Protagonist remains still while crowd movement creates a time-lapse contrast. Keep signage generic or accurately rendered; avoid malformed prominent text. Choose and lock one specific station design before producing repeated shots.

## Story props
- Diary: contemporary A5 hardcover; matte charcoal/black/navy cover and cream/off-white pages are provisional until a reference is approved.
- Laptop and phone: contemporary, understated, consistent across desk shots.
- Hackathon award: solo achievement; exact trophy/medal/certificate design pending.
- Job offer and medical reports: legible enough to communicate the story, but avoid generated gibberish text as a focal point; use post-production inserts for exact readable wording if needed.
- University admission and loan rejection: use designed inserts or carefully controlled close-ups rather than relying on AI-generated small text.
- Breakup/job departure: two coffee cups, turned-down photograph, access card or packed desk box may be used as visual evidence; select exact props during shot design.
