# Project Brief — Ek Ek Lost

## Objective
Create a cohesive, cinematic AI-assisted music video for the completed Hindi song “Ek Ek Lost”. The target is a professional short-film experience, not a montage of unrelated generated clips.

## Reconstruction contract
A future AI or filmmaker must be able to recreate the visual film from scratch using this repository and the exact song audio supplied separately. No access to this conversation, current AI platform, current-production images, face references, moodboards, location photos, generated clips or external media archive may be required.

The future AI must generate its own character sheets, environment references and prop references from the written bibles in this repository, then use those internally to create the shots. Current-production reference images such as CH3 are optional aids for this production only.

## Required external input
The exact song audio master will be supplied later as an audio file or accessible download link. GitHub remains text-only and will not store the audio file. The repository contains the lyrics and manually authored timing markers; the audio itself is a separate required input.

## Production constraints
- Zero budget: use genuinely free tools/features; label limited credits and paid tiers honestly.
- Generate images/video externally. ChatGPT supports creative direction, prompts, continuity, production planning and editing guidance; it does not generate the project's assets.
- DaVinci Resolve Free is the intended editor. Keep the workflow practical for a Windows PC with 8 GB RAM and a 4 GB Radeon GPU; prioritize 1080p, proxies and short clips.
- Preserve reusable, model-agnostic master prompts. Keep model-specific adaptations separate.
- Do not commit images, videos, audio or project binaries to GitHub.

## Story in one sentence
A talented young man gradually loses confidence, dreams, relationships and identity, while his mother still sees the person he was before life's hardships; her remembered blessing helps him find the strength to begin again.

## Working principles
1. Music timing is authoritative; use the supplied Audacity labels and verify against the actual supplied audio.
2. Every shot must carry a narrative or emotional purpose.
3. Maintain strict character, wardrobe, prop and location continuity.
4. Prefer achievable short AI clips over complex actions.
5. Do not silently alter the approved lyrics or story decisions.
6. No prompt may require an image or reference outside this repository.
